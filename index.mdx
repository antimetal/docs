Antimetal handles everything that happens after you deploy. It investigates, fixes, and prevents issues by connecting your cloud platforms, observability tools, and code.

- **Diagnose** - When an alert fires, Antimetal pulls together data from your logs, metrics, traces, code, and incident history to identify the root cause.
- **Fix** - Antimetal generates production-ready fixes tied to evidence, so your team can review, validate, and ship.
- **Prevent** - [Patrols](/product/patrols) check your systems on a schedule and surface blind spots and inefficiencies before they become incidents.
